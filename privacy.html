Privacy and terms

Last updated 16 October 2026. Abdur Rafay, abdurrafaym10@gmail.com

Short version

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics. I only learn about you if you contact me.

What happens when you contact me

  • Contact form: your name, email, subject and message are sent to me by email through FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, which processes them only to deliver the message.
  • Email: I receive your name, email address and message. I use them only to reply to you, and I delete them on request.
  • WhatsApp: the WhatsApp button opens a chat with me in WhatsApp. That conversation follows WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

What stays in your browser

  • The digit recognizer: the digits you draw are read by a neural network that runs on your device. Your drawing is never sent anywhere and is gone when you close the pad.
  • The host's voice: pre-recorded audio of my own text (a few lines that include your name use your browser's built-in speech feature instead). It never listens to you.

Services this site loads

  • Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any web host, it handles standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and stop abuse.
  • jsDelivr delivers the 3D library (three.js), and Google Fonts delivers the fonts. Your browser contacts them directly, so they can see your IP address.

  • Project descriptions are my own work, described as accurately as I can. Details covered by confidentiality are left out on purpose.
  • The site is provided as is. I try to keep it accurate and working, but I cannot guarantee that it always will be.
  • Links to LinkedIn, GitHub and WhatsApp lead to sites with their own terms.
